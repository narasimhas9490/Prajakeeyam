Delete your Prajakeeyam account

You can delete your account and the personal data attached to it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open ప్రజాకీయం (Prajakeeyam).
  2. Tap the profile icon at the top right.
  3. Tap Delete my account (నా ఖాతాను తొలగించు) and confirm.

This happens immediately.

By e-mail

Send a message from the Google e-mail address you used to sign in to CONTACT_EMAIL with the subject “Delete my Prajakeeyam account”. We will delete it within 7 days and confirm by e-mail.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, e-mail address, profile picture and Google account link are removed from our database.
  • Your home village setting is removed.
  • Every problem and comment you posted is hidden from all users.

Server backups are rotated within 30 days. See the privacy policy for details.
