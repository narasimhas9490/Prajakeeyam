ప్రజాకీయం (Prajakeeyam) – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

ప్రజాకీయం ఒక స్వతంత్ర, పార్టీలకు అతీతమైన పౌర యాప్. ఇది ఏ రాజకీయ పార్టీకి లేదా ప్రభుత్వానికి చెందినది కాదు.

Prajakeeyam is an independent, non-partisan civic app for Andhra Pradesh. It lets people post the problems of their village or mandal so that everyone can see them. It is not linked to any political party or government body.

What we collect

  • Google account details when you sign in with Google: your name, e-mail address and profile picture. We use these only to identify your account and show your name next to what you post.
  • Content you post: problem titles, descriptions, comments, upvotes, reports and the photos you attach. Posts, comments and photos are public and visible to every user of the app together with your display name.
  • Your chosen village/mandal/constituency. This is stored on your phone, and on our server only if you set a home village in your profile.

Location: if you allow it, the app reads your phone's location once to suggest your village. The lookup happens on your phone (Android's built-in geocoder); your coordinates are never sent to or stored on our server. You can always pick your village manually instead.

We do not collect contacts, phone number, or advertising identifiers, and we do not run advertising or analytics SDKs.

How it is used

To show problems by village, mandal and constituency; to let you support, comment on and report posts; and to moderate abuse (reports, blocks, hiding content, suspending accounts that break the rules).

Where it is stored (service providers)

  • Render hosts the API server.
  • Supabase hosts the database (account details, posts, comments).
  • Cloudinary stores the photos you attach to posts.
  • Google provides sign-in (Google Sign-In).

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with anyone other than these providers, who process it only to run the app.

Your choices

  • Browsing the app never requires an account.
  • You can delete your own posts at any time from the post menu.
  • You can delete your account from Profile → Delete my account in the app, or by following the steps on the account deletion page. Deleting your account removes your name, e-mail and picture from our database and hides everything you posted.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
