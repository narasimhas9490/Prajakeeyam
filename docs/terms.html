Prajakeeyam – Terms of Use

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Prajakeeyam is a public notice board for village problems in Andhra Pradesh. By posting you agree to the rules below.

Rules for posting

  • Post only real problems about public services and infrastructure (roads, water, electricity, drainage, health, schools, ration, pensions, garbage and similar).
  • No abuse, hate speech, threats, caste or religious attacks, or harassment of any person.
  • No personal data of other people (phone numbers, addresses, photos of private individuals without consent).
  • No political party campaigning, advertising, spam or repeated posting of the same problem.
  • No false or misleading claims. Photos must be of the problem you are reporting.

Moderation

Anyone can report a post or comment and block a user. Content reported by several users is hidden automatically until a moderator reviews it. Moderators may hide content, change a problem's status, or suspend accounts that break these rules. There is no guarantee that a posted problem will be fixed by anyone: the app only makes problems visible.

Your content

You keep ownership of what you post. You give Prajakeeyam a licence to display it publicly in the app. You can delete your own posts and your account at any time.

No affiliation

Prajakeeyam is an independent civic project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for any political party, candidate, or government department.

Liability

The app is provided as is, free of charge, without warranties. We are not responsible for user-generated content or for actions taken by third parties based on it.
